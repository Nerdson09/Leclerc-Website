Start of F1 career

Charles Leclerc's Formula 1 career began in 2018 when he joined the Sauber team, which was later rebranded as Alfa Romeo Racing. In his debut season, Leclerc impressed many with his performances, consistently outperforming his more experienced teammate and earning the "Rookie of the Year" title. His standout performance came at the Belgian Grand Prix, where he finished in 6th place, showcasing his potential as a future star in the sport.


Move to Ferrari

In 2019, Charles Leclerc made a significant move to Scuderia Ferrari, one of the most prestigious teams in Formula 1. This move was seen as a major step in his career, and he quickly adapted to his new team. Leclerc's first season with Ferrari was impressive, and earned him 4th place in the driver's chamionship, only 1 spot behind the future 3 time world champion Max Verstappen. He secured his first Formula 1 victory at the Belgian Grand Prix, and followed it up with another win at the Italian Grand Prix, making him a fan favorite and a rising star in the sport.


Recent Seasons

In the following seasons, Charles Leclerc continued to show his talent and potential, consistently finishing in the top positions and challenging for podiums. However, he has faced challenges with the performance of the Ferrari car, which has struggled to compete with the top teams. Despite this, Leclerc has remained a key figure in the team and continues to be a strong contender in the championship. As of 2024, Charles Leclerc is considered one of the most promising young drivers in Formula 1, and fans are eager to see how his career will unfold in the coming years.
